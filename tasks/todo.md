**What landed.** One defect class across three branches: an AppleScript `try` with no `on error` arm, which converts a loud failure into an exit-0 wrong answer. `run_applescript` raises on a nonzero `osascript` exit, so a script with *no* `try` is correct; the bare `try` is the bug. Alongside it: the enforced public-repo identity gate (AGENTIC-2358), the `search_emails` subject-fast-path date-window fix (AGENTIC-2356), bounded-scan fallback conversion (AGENTIC-2355), `manage_trash` `dry_run` / `max_deletes` / date-window bounds (AGENTIC-2359), two file-clobber paths (AGENTIC-2361), timeout validation (AGENTIC-2369), and non-positive action caps (AGENTIC-2374).

The two ratchets also collided on key namespace: #90 widened `test_no_unbounded_whose.py` from `TOOLS_DIR` to `PACKAGE_DIR` while #91 ratcheted in the old namespace; the entries are restated under the current namespace with a comment recording why.
